## Camera selection policy

This is an option library, not a checklist or a source of default scenes. Infer a stable capture language from the account's `vibe`, `content_angle`, narrative beat, and the niche's editorial territory. For each post, select only the genre, distance, position, focus, and optional treatment that serve that moment; do not copy locations, props, or actions from the descriptions below.

Use a natural mobile perspective as the baseline. Wide, aerial, distorted, analogue, and strongly stylized treatments are occasional editorial experiments, never generic phone-realism tokens and never a requirement for reference portraits. Keep the account recognizable by repeating its visual logic, then vary the selected treatment when the story earns it. Do not stack competing lens, focus, or angle instructions.

## Photo Genres

| Genre | Prompt tag | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Macrophotography | `macro photography` | Extreme close-up of tiny details — textures, pores, fabric weave, jewelry details; background fully dissolved |
| Still Life | `still life photo` | Arranged inanimate objects — product shots, cosmetics, accessories on a surface; controlled composition |
| Aerial Photography | `aerial photography` | Shot from aircraft/drone — geography, architecture, crowds, landscape scale |
| Panoramic | `panoramic photo` | Ultra-wide horizontal sweep — vistas, cityscapes, group scenes; stitched wide-angle feel |
| Landscape | `landscape photo` | Natural or urban scenery as subject — environment tells the story, human presence optional |
| Astrophotography | `astrophotography` | Night sky, stars, Milky Way — long-exposure celestial scenes; requires dark environment |
| Flat Lay | `flat lay photography` | Objects arranged on a surface, shot directly from above — products, food, outfits, cosmetics |
| Wildlife | `wildlife photography` | Animals in natural habitat — telephoto reach, patience, environmental context |
| Underwater | `underwater photography` | Below water surface — pools, oceans, submerged subjects; light distortion, bubbles |
| Action Scene | `action scene photo` | High-energy moment with motion blur and dynamic background — sports, running, splashes |
| Portrait | `portrait photo` | Person as primary subject — face and expression dominant; personality over environment |
| Drone Photo | `drone photo` | Captured by UAV — aerial versatility, mid-altitude perspectives, real-estate / travel / event coverage |
| Lifestyle | `lifestyle photo` | Candid everyday moment styled to feel real — morning routine, walking the dog, cooking; authenticity over polish |
| Street Style | `street style photo` | Fashion captured in an urban environment; candid wardrobe showcase |
| Boudoir | `boudoir photography` | Intimate indoor setting — bedroom, soft light, lingerie/cozywear; sensual but tasteful, private atmosphere |
| Behind the Scenes | `behind the scenes photo` | Candid documentary of a process — backstage, prep, mirror, makeup chair; raw unpolished authenticity |
| Editorial | `editorial photo` | Magazine-style fashion — dramatic styling, intentional art direction, high-end aesthetic; story-driven not product-driven |
| Candid | `candid photo` | Subject unposed, caught in the moment — laughing, talking, walking; naturalness over composition, no camera awareness |
| Lookbook | `lookbook photo` | Outfit-focused series against clean or minimal background — catalog-style wardrobe showcase, consistent framing across set |
| Travel | `travel photo` | On-location lifestyle; the destination provides context while the subject explores or relaxes |
| Mirror Selfie | `mirror selfie` | OOTD (Outfit of the Day) shot via mirror reflection — phone visible, casual wardrobe log, bathroom or full-length mirror; authentic daily style |

## Camera Distance (Shot Size)

| Shot | Prompt tag | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Macro Shot | `macro shot` | Extremely close via macro lens — single detail fills frame, background fully blurred; texture, pores, jewelry |
| Extreme Close-Up (ECU) | `extreme close-up` | Frame filled with a single detail (eyes, mouth, lips) — maximum intimacy / intensity / discomfort |
| Close-Up (CU) | `close-up` | Frame filled with character's face — pure emotion and reaction, important moments |
| Selfie Shot | `selfie shot` | Model holds camera herself — arm extension implied, casual angle, phone-camera aesthetic |
| Medium Close-Up (MCU) | `medium close-up` | Chest/shoulders up — bridge between medium and close-up; build toward emotional climax, body language + face |
| Cowboy Shot | `cowboy shot` | From mid-thigh/hips up — show holsters, belts, hands-on-hips; western-derived but universal for hip-level action |
| Medium Shot (MS) | `medium shot` | Waist up through torso — most common dialog shot; subject emphasized, surroundings still visible |
| Medium Long Shot (MLS) | `medium long shot` / `medium wide shot` | Character from knees up — dialog scenes, body language + setting balance |
| Full Body Shot (FS) | `full body shot` / `full shot` | Entire body fills the frame, emphasis on character — show posture, outfit, full-body action |
| Wide Shot (WS/LS) | `wide shot` / `long shot` | Full body with plenty of surrounding space — establish geography, character-in-environment |
| Extreme Wide Shot (ELS) | `extreme wide shot` | Subject tiny vs vast location — landscapes, isolation, scale, "how small the person feels" |
| Establishing Shot | `establishing shot` | Not a size — a function: opens a scene, shows location, sets tone. No central subject, all figures small |
| Master Shot | `master shot` | Object is the center of the story but camera captures wide context — full scene geography with clear subject |

## Camera Position (Angles & Orientation)

| Position | Prompt tag | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Eye Level | `eye-level shot` | Neutral perspective, matches human vision — default for most scenes, no power dynamic |
| Shoulder Level | `shoulder-level shot` | Camera at shoulder height — slightly shorter than eye level; model framed chest-up; common for dialog |
| Hip Level | `hip-level shot` | Camera at hip height — hands-on-hips action, western standoffs, grounded body language |
| Knee Level | `knee-level shot, at distance` | Camera at knee height — paired with low angle amplifies superiority; add `at distance` to avoid framing only knees |
| Ground Level | `ground-level shot` | Camera on the ground — crawling, falling, grounded action, street-level realism |
| Low Angle / Bottom View | `low-angle shot` / `bottom view` | Camera below subject looking up — power, dominance, heroism, intimidation |
| High Angle | `high-angle shot` | Camera above subject looking down — vulnerability, inferiority, smallness; context can shift meaning |
| Overhead Shot | `overhead shot` | Camera from forehead of model, looking down — beauty/grooming angles, hair styling, face products |
| Shot From Above | `shot from above` | Camera positioned above model — fashion editorial, dramatic top-down perspective |
| Bird's-Eye View | `bird's-eye view` | Directly overhead, high altitude — scale, movement patterns, god's-eye geography; flat-lays |
| Aerial Shot | `aerial shot` / `drone shot` | Highest vantage — vast landscapes, sweeping motion, epic establishing |
| Dutch Angle | `dutch angle shot` / `canted angle` | Camera tilted sideways, horizon slanted — disorientation, unease, tension, psychological instability |
| Point of View (POV) | `POV shot` | Camera at operator's eye level — first-person perspective, viewer is in the scene |
| Over-The-Shoulder (OTS) | `over-the-shoulder shot` | Camera behind model — adds depth, `character is looking over the shoulder` for narrative framing |
| Side View | `side view` / `profile shot` | Camera at 90° to subject — profile silhouette, editorial fashion, dramatic jawline |
| From the Back | `back view` / `rear shot` / `from behind` | Camera behind model — mystery, departure, outfit-back detail, walking-away narrative |
| Front View | `front view` | Camera directly in front — confrontational, symmetrical, direct engagement |
| Mugshot | `mugshot style` | Strictly frontal, no angles — raw, documentary, stark lighting; edgy fashion / character study |
| Shoot Through | `shoot through leaves` / `shoot through foreground` | Camera frames subject through an obstacle (leaves, glass, fabric) — layered depth, voyeuristic, cinematic |

## Depth of Field (Focus)

| Focus | Prompt tag | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Shallow Focus | `shallow focus, blurred background` | Subject sharp, background blurry — direct the eye to one element, convey intimacy or isolation |
| Deep Focus | `deep focus, everything in focus` | Foreground + midground + background all sharp — audience reads the whole scene; realism, scale |
| Soft Focus | `soft focus` | Nothing is razor-sharp — dream, memory, nostalgia; flattering portraits, warm hazy mood |
| Sharp Focus | `sharp focus` | Subject tack-sharp, maximum detail — product photography, texture showcase, clinical clarity |
| Tilt-Shift | `tilt-shift` | Selective focus via lens rotation — miniature/diorama effect, dreamlike distortion, surreal scale |
| Split Diopter | `split diopter focus` | Two focal planes sharp simultaneously (foreground + background, middle blurred) — unnatural, use sparingly |
| Focus Stacking | `focus stacking` | Multiple photos merged for full depth — every detail sharp front-to-back; macro/product photography |

## Combinations examples (angle + size + focus in one tag)

For prompt engineering, combine genre + size + position + focus + equipment in a single phrase:

- `extreme wide shot, high-angle, deep focus` — epic landscape establishing
- `close-up, eye-level, shallow focus` — intimate emotional portrait
- `medium shot, low-angle, shallow focus` — heroic character introduction
- `full shot, dutch angle, deep focus` — unsettling full-body tension
- `extreme close-up, eye-level, shallow focus` — maximum emotional intensity
- `wide shot, aerial, deep focus` — god's-eye scale and geography
- `medium close-up, shoulder-level, soft focus` — nostalgic dreamlike dialog
- `cowboy shot, hip-level, shallow focus` — standoff / hands-on-hips attitude
- `ground-level shot, low-angle, deep focus` — grounded heroism looking up
- `medium wide shot, high-angle, tilt-shift` — surreal miniature-like scene
- `selfie shot, eye-level, shallow focus, Polaroid instant photo` — candid party snapshot
- `portrait photo, over-the-shoulder shot, shallow focus, Kodak Portra 400` — editorial warmth
- `flat lay photography, bird's-eye view, sharp focus, Kodak Ektar 100` — vivid product flat lay
- `macro shot, side view, sharp focus, Ilford HP5 Plus` — dramatic B&W texture detail
- `full body shot, from behind, medium shot, soft focus, Fujifilm Superia 400` — walking-away street style
