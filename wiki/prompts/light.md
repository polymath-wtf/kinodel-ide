## Light selection policy

This is an option library, not a checklist. First select a plausible light source, quality, direction, and temperature for the narrative beat. Then select only the colour treatment or effect that supports the account's visual language. Do not copy a scene from an entry or stack unrelated effects.

Natural, available-looking light is the normal raw-phone baseline. Strongly stylized effects, professional lighting setups, and deliberate optical distortion are occasional editorial experiments when the account's content angle gives them a clear purpose. They are never default phone-realism markers and do not belong in reference portraits.
