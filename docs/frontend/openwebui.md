# Open WebUI Reference

Status: **Reference only**

Kinodel uses its own [Pipeline / Chat workspace](webui.md) and [wireframe](uiux-wireframe.md). Open WebUI may inform chat ergonomics, attachment handling and reconnect behavior; it is not the product shell or a required dependency.

Do not inherit its architecture or database contracts. Kinodel's Chat is another view of the same execution, exact reviews and owner-bound discussion. It shares result cards and command handlers with Pipeline; it does not introduce a global agent or a second conversation runtime. Library/search and timeline editing follow their own later product scope.
