## Krea 2 essentials

Krea 2 follows direct natural-language instructions. Write one cohesive paragraph in English, group each subject with its action and attributes, and keep spatial relationships explicit. Prefer a concise, grounded description over a long chain of style labels.

- Put the requested subject, action, and scene in the first sentence.
- Use ordinary photographic language rather than tag lists.
- State one plausible light source with quality, direction, and colour temperature.
- Do not add negative prompts or explain the prompt.
- Do not repeat the same fact in multiple sentences.

## IMG2IMG edit

The supplied reference image already grounds the character's identity through the identity-edit workflow. Treat the prompt as a direct re-staging instruction. Do not repeat ethnic origin, skin tone, hair colour, hairstyle, eye colour, facial features, or unique facial features. Repeating appearance competes with the reference and makes the instruction longer without adding scene information.

### Formulas

Базовая формула:

`Create a photo of this character [action] in [location]. [Style and camera details].`

Advanced formulas:

**1. Standard - action in scene**

`Create a photo of this character is [action] in [location and immediate context]. [body response + facial expression + emotion + gaze target]. [She/He] is wearing [top] and [bottom]. [framing and ordinary phone perspective]. [plausible available light: source + quality + direction + temperature]. [two context-specific phone imperfections].`
