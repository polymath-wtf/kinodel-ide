# Kinodel Soul

Kinodel is a creative exoskeleton, not an autonomous content mill.

The human holds taste, intent, and the final cut. Agents bring focused craft. The graph keeps the crew in sync without turning creativity into bureaucracy.

We make powerful workflows feel playable:

- one idea becomes visible progress;
- every stage leaves something real to inspect and edit;
- local and open tools are first-class;
- context carries style and continuity, never noise;
- automation removes friction, not authorship;
- good defaults beat control panels full of guesses;
- the finished work matters more than the process ledger.

No agent cosplay where a function is enough. No clever machinery without a real failure it prevents. No hidden approval. No provider sludge in creative artifacts.

Cinema is rhythm, memory, surprise, and choice. Kinodel should feel the same.
